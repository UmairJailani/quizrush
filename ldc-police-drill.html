  • Reasoning traps: in series questions, check differences of differences before giving up (e.g. 2,6,12,20,30 → gaps 4,6,8,10).
  • Syllogism: only trust what's forced by the given statements — not what sounds true from general knowledge.
  • Computer: CPU = Central Processing Unit, RAM is volatile (wiped on power-off), F5 refreshes, Ctrl+J justifies, Ctrl+F finds.
  • Jumbled words: count the letters given against each option before picking one — misspelled look-alikes and "almost right" words are the deliberate trap.
  • English: "Neither/Either" takes a singular verb; watch subject-verb agreement traps.
  • Math: speed = distance ÷ time (convert minutes to hours); discount original price = sale price ÷ (1 − discount%).
  • Pak Studies: Objectives Resolution — 1949; Balochistan is the largest province by area.
  • Islamiat: 5 Pillars; Quran's revelation began in Ramadan; 1st Caliph = Hazrat Abu Bakr (RA).
  • Skip a hard one fast (mark it), finish the easy ones first — with no negative marking, coverage beats perfectionism.
